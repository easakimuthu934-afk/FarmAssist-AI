# 5-Minute Pitch Script

## 0:00–0:35 | Hook
"A farmer may know exactly what happened in the field today, but that information is often split across notebooks, phone messages and memory. The result is a simple problem: the right information is not in one place when a decision has to be made. FarmAssist AI brings the farm diary, finance and decision support together."

## 0:35–1:15 | Problem
"Farmers need to track crops, field work, irrigation, observations, costs and harvests. Connectivity can also be unreliable. Existing tools can be too fragmented or too complex. FarmAssist is designed around one question: what is the most useful action for this farm today?"

## 1:15–2:45 | Live demo
1. Click **Load demo farm**.
2. Show the dashboard: active crop, season cost, revenue and profit/loss.
3. Open **My Farm** and show the crop stage calculated from planting date.
4. Open **Reports** and show a daily field report with condition and observations.
5. Open **Finance** and show costs and harvest revenue.
6. Open **Analytics** to show expense breakdown.
7. Open **FarmAssist AI** and click **Today**.

## 2:45–3:40 | AI explanation
"The decision copilot does not guess field facts. It reads structured signals already recorded by the farmer: crop type and growth stage, recent field observations, weather risk and financial records. It then produces explainable recommendations such as checking soil moisture, reviewing pest observations or watching drainage when rain risk is elevated. This makes the AI layer auditable and useful even before a hosted generative model is added."

## 3:40–4:20 | Why this approach
"FarmAssist is offline-first, multilingual and built around a simple data model. The core records remain usable without a connection. Weather updates are retrieved when online and the latest successful result is cached. English, Tamil and Hindi are supported."

## 4:20–5:00 | Impact + roadmap
"The MVP proves the workflow from field record to decision. The next version can add secure cloud sync, production authentication, speech-to-text for hands-free field reporting, image-based crop issue analysis and a hosted LLM for richer natural-language reasoning. FarmAssist AI is not trying to replace the farmer. It is trying to make the farmer's own information easier to use at the moment it matters."
